# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Project Name

Bagicha

Tagline:
"Bring Nature Home"

---

# Product Vision

Bagicha is a modern e-commerce platform that allows customers to purchase plants, gardening tools, seeds, fertilizers, planters, and gardening accessories online.

The platform focuses on providing an enjoyable shopping experience while educating users about proper plant care.

---

# Problem Statement

Traditional nurseries have several limitations:

- Limited plant variety
- No online ordering
- Poor inventory visibility
- Lack of plant care information
- Difficult comparison between products

Bagicha solves these issues through a modern digital shopping experience.

---
